houndie 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-10-26

houndie 是一款帮助外贸商家接待海外客户、制作视频并投放 Facebook 广告的应用。本页说明我们处理哪些数据、用来做什么，以及你如何撤销授权和要求删除数据。

1. 我们处理的数据

  • Facebook 授权信息。你授权后，我们通过 Meta Marketing API 访问你的广告账户和公共主页，用于创建、暂停、开启广告，以及读取花费、展示、点击等效果数据。我们保存访问令牌，用于代你执行这些操作。
  • 广告素材和设置。你在应用内选择的视频、封面、文案，以及你填写的预算、投放天数和投放国家。
  • 客户咨询消息。你把自己的 WhatsApp 号码连接到 houndie 后，我们会处理该号码收到的客户咨询消息，仅用于为你提供自动接待和客户管理。
  • 账号信息。登录用的手机号。

2. 我们如何使用这些数据

只用于向你提供上述功能。我们不出售你的数据，也不会把你的数据用于与服务无关的广告或画像。

3. 数据保存与共享

访问令牌和业务数据保存在我们控制的服务器上。除下列情形外，我们不会向第三方披露：为提供服务而必须调用的第三方接口（例如 Meta 的接口），或法律要求。

4. 撤销授权

你可以随时在 Facebook 的「设置 → 应用和网站」中移除 houndie，我们将无法再访问你的广告账户和主页。

5. 数据删除

如需删除我们保存的与你相关的数据（包括访问令牌、广告记录、客户消息记录），请发送邮件到下方联系邮箱，写明你登录用的手机号。我们会删除与该账号关联的数据，并回复确认。

6. 联系我们

houndie Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

houndie is an app that helps exporters handle overseas customer inquiries, produce videos, and run Facebook ads. This page explains what data we process, why, and how you can revoke access or request deletion.

1. Data we process

  • Facebook authorization. With your permission, we access your ad account and Page through the Meta Marketing API to create, pause and start ads and to read performance data such as spend, impressions and clicks. We store the access token to perform these actions on your behalf.
  • Ad assets and settings. The videos, cover images and text you select, plus the budget, duration and target countries you enter.
  • Customer inquiry messages. If you connect your WhatsApp number to houndie, we process the customer messages that number receives, only to provide automated reception and customer management to you.
  • Account information. The phone number you use to sign in.

2. How we use data

Only to provide the features above. We do not sell your data and do not use it for unrelated advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and sharing

Access tokens and business data are stored on servers we control. We do not disclose data to third parties except for third-party APIs needed to provide the service (such as Meta's APIs), or where required by law.

4. Revoking access

You can remove houndie at any time in Facebook under Settings → Apps and Websites. After that, we can no longer access your ad account or Page.

5. Data deletion

To request deletion of the data we hold about you (including access tokens, ad records and customer message records), email the address below with the phone number you sign in with. We will delete the data linked to that account and confirm by reply.
